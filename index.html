Introduction

I am currently an M.S. graduate student at POSTECH, focusing on developing secure and reliable AI systems for cyber-physical systems, including robotics and drones. My research aims to address security challenges like GPS spoofing to ensure robust and trustworthy operations in critical environments. My research interests include trustworthy machine learning, conformal prediction, vision-language-action models, and cyber-physical systems. My advisor is Sangdon Park, an assistant professor at POSTECH.
